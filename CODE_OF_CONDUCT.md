# Code of Conduct

We want technical discussions and collaboration to remain professional, constructive, and safe for contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Give technically specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good faith while still challenging unsafe or incorrect changes.
- Respect differing levels of experience and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, and disclosure of private or security-sensitive information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately with the maintainers rather than escalated through public project threads.
